Drain Cleaning in Round Rock, TX

We clear slow, gurgling, and backing-up drains across Round Rock.

Call (512) 882-1291 Ask a question

Water backing into a shower is a drain warning. A gurgling sink, standing water, or a sour odor near a floor drain can point to the same line.

A blockage may sit in the trap, branch pipe, or a cleanout farther along the route. Drain trouble should not be left alone because grease, soap, food, and hair can compact inside the pipe and turn a slow fixture into a backup.

Gurgling sinks and drain odors

We hear about sinks that drain only after several minutes, tubs that fill while the washing machine empties, and toilets that bubble when another fixture runs. Kitchen sinks may hold water under the basket strainer, while a shower can send water across the bathroom floor.

A sewer-like smell can come from a dry trap, trapped debris, or a blockage farther down the branch line. These symptoms can look alike from the room, but each fault has its own access, tools, and scope once we inspect the pipe.

Drain line inspection

We check the fixture, trap, branch line, and reachable cleanout before selecting a cable, auger, or water flush. We note whether a 1 1/2-inch sink line, 2-inch shower line, or larger main is involved.

A basic flow test and trap check take about 10 to 15 minutes. Clearing one accessible fixture line often takes under an hour, and we remove hair, soap, food residue, or grease where the blockage is reachable.

We can usually clear an accessible line and test its flow during the same visit. We replace a damaged trap washer or cleanout cap if inspection shows it is needed, while camera work, buried pipe work, or sewer line repair may need separate scheduling.

Cleanouts, pipe material, and access

An accessible cleanout near a kitchen or bathroom gives the cable a direct route. A buried, painted-over, or seized cleanout cap adds locating and opening work before the line can be cleared.

Grease in a kitchen branch, hair in a shower trap, and roots in an outdoor line need different tools and clearing time. Older cast-iron pipe can hold scale that catches debris again after a basic cable pass.

The pipe material, route, and need for a camera affect the scope. A nonstandard cleanout fitting or damaged trap piece may need to be ordered, and any permit or sign-off for pipe alteration is addressed before that work is scheduled.

Fixture location and cleanout

Before calling, note the room and fixture with the problem, such as the kitchen sink, hall bathroom tub, or laundry drain. Tell us whether water is standing now and which other fixture causes the backup.

If you can see a cleanout, describe where it sits and whether furniture, cabinets, or landscaping block access. For a kitchen sink, the garbage disposal make and model on its label can also help us identify the setup.

Keep any prior camera footage or plumbing invoice nearby if the line has backed up before. Call Round Rock Plumbing Company at (512) 882-1291 before a partial blockage packs tighter and sends wastewater back through the fixture.
