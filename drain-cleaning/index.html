Drain Cleaning in Round Rock, TX

Clearing slow, gurgling, and backed-up drains across Round Rock.

Call (512) 882-1291 Ask a question

A sink that gurgles, a shower that holds water, or a drain with a sewage smell points to a blockage or venting issue. Grease, soap residue, hair, and mineral scale can narrow the pipe until water cannot move normally.

We clear drains in Round Rock, Brushy Creek, Windemere, and Wells Branch. A slow drain in one fixture can sometimes wait, but water backing into a tub, shower, or floor drain should not wait because wastewater can overflow into the room.

Gurgling sink and tub backup

Customers often report a kitchen sink that drains slowly, a shower pan filling around their feet, or a bathroom sink that makes a gurgling sound. Other common signs include a floor drain with standing water, a foul smell from a fixture, and water rising in one drain while another fixture runs.

A clog in a trap, a blocked branch line, and a restricted vent can produce similar sounds and backups. The repair work and materials differ, so we inspect the fixture and pipe route before identifying what needs clearing.

Fixture inspection and cable work

We start at the affected sink, tub, shower, or floor drain. We check the trap, stopper, strainer, drain opening, cleanout, and visible pipe joints. We run water to see how quickly the fixture drains and note whether another drain reacts.

A basic trap check may take about 15 minutes. Cable cleaning for an accessible branch line often takes 30 to 90 minutes, depending on the blockage and pipe route. We can usually clear an accessible clog during the visit, and may replace a worn trap washer or damaged cleanout plug if needed. A blockage beyond the available cleanout, or one requiring a camera inspection, may need a separate visit with the right equipment.

Cleanout access and pipe route

The cleanout location affects the work. A cleanout in a garage, utility room, or exterior wall gives us a more direct path than a line reached only through a sink trap or tight cabinet.

Pipe material also matters. Older cast-iron pipe can collect scale inside the wall of the line, while plastic pipe may have a different fitting layout or access point. A seized cleanout plug, a buried access point, or a blockage deep in a long branch line adds labor and may require additional equipment.

Fixture and cleanout location

Tell us which room and fixture is draining slowly or backing up. Let us know whether the kitchen sink, shower, tub, bathroom sink, or floor drain is affected.

Check whether water is standing in the basin, pan, or floor drain now. Note if running the dishwasher, washing machine, or another faucet makes the problem appear in a different fixture.

If you know where the cleanout is, mention whether it is outside, in a garage, under a cabinet, or behind an access panel. A photo of the drain opening, stopper, or visible pipe can also help us prepare.
