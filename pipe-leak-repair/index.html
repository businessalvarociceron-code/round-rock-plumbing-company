Pipe Leak Repair in Round Rock, TX

We trace and repair leaking pipes before water damages walls, cabinets, and floors.

Call (512) 882-1291 Ask a question

A slow drip under a sink can leave a cabinet floor swollen within a week. A small wet spot on drywall can spread along a stud bay over a month, loosening paint, staining ceilings, and wetting insulation.

Water can also escape behind a shower wall or beneath a slab without a visible puddle. Pipe leaks should not wait because moisture keeps moving through framing, drywall, flooring, and cabinets until the source is isolated.

Water stains and hissing

Customers often call about a brown ceiling ring, a damp cabinet base, or a hissing sound behind a wall. Other signs include paint bubbling near a baseboard, a warm patch on a floor, or a water meter that moves after every fixture is off.

A drip at a supply connection, a split pipe joint, and a leak inside a wall can look similar from the room side. We inspect the pipe and fitting because each fault needs a different repair and can involve different parts and access.

Leak inspection and pressure test

We start a leak inspection by checking the water meter, visible supply lines, shutoff valves, fixture connections, cabinets, and nearby drywall. We isolate sections of piping where possible and use a pressure gauge to see whether the system loses pressure with faucets closed.

A basic exposed-leak check can take about 15 to 30 minutes. Finding a concealed leak can take longer because we trace moisture through wall cavities, ceiling framing, or flooring before opening a surface.

We can usually replace an exposed supply line, valve, compression fitting, or accessible section of pipe on the same visit. A pipe run behind tile, inside a wall, below a slab, or requiring ordered material is scheduled for return work after the leak location and access route are confirmed.

Pipe access and shutoff valves

The pipe material, fitting age, and location of the leak affect the scope of repair. A corroded copper joint, brittle plastic fitting, or damaged supply line may need a different connection method and replacement part.

Easy access under a sink or in a utility room keeps the work focused on the failed section. A leak behind tile, drywall, cabinets, or insulation requires more time to reach and test.

A seized shutoff valve can add work because water must be controlled before a fitting is removed. Parts that need ordering, as well as any required inspection or sign-off, also change the work involved.

Fixture room and pipe material

Tell us which room has the wet wall, stain, hissing sound, or damp cabinet. Let us know which fixture is closest, such as a sink, shower, washing machine, or refrigerator line.

If you can see the pipe, note whether it is copper, plastic, or braided supply hose. Check whether water is actively running and whether the shutoff valve or access panel is reachable.

A photo of the fitting and the surrounding cabinet or wall can help us prepare for the visit. Call Round Rock Plumbing Company at (512) 882-1291 before a continuing leak turns a small wet area into damaged drywall, flooring, or framing.
