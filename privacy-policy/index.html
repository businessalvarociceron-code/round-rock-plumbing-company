Privacy Policy

This page explains what this website collects and what happens to it. It describes this website only.

What the site collects

Nothing is collected automatically beyond the standard request information your browser sends to the host — the page requested, the time, and a general location derived from the connection. No analytics or advertising scripts run on this site.

If you use the contact form, the details you type are sent to the business and used to answer you. They are not sold and not passed to anyone else.

The map

The location map is embedded from Google Maps. Loading a page that contains the map means your browser contacts Google, and Google may set cookies and record the request. That is Google’s processing, under Google’s privacy policy, and it happens whether or not you interact with the map.
