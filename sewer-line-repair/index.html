Sewer Line Repair in Round Rock, TX

Camera-led sewer line repairs for backups, odors, and damaged pipe in Round Rock.

Call (512) 882-1291 Ask a question

Older homes in Round Rock and Brushy Creek can still have cast-iron, clay, or Orangeburg sewer pipe beneath the yard or slab. These materials can crack, shift at joints, or lose their shape as soil moves and roots enter the line.

Newer properties can also develop trouble at a pipe connection, cleanout, or low section of the yard line. We inspect the pipe route and the fixtures affected before recommending repair work.

A damaged sewer line should not be left for later. A crack or separation can release wastewater into soil, bring sewage through a floor drain, and allow a backup to spread farther through the home.

Sewage smell and yard puddles

Customers often call about a sewage smell near the bathroom, a wet patch in the yard, or water bubbling from a cleanout cap. Other common reports include toilets that gurgle after flushing and a tub or shower that fills with dirty water.

A backup in one fixture can point to a blockage, but repeated trouble across several fixtures often involves the main line. Soft ground above the pipe can also signal a broken joint, though irrigation and rainwater can look similar.

These signs can come from different faults, including root entry, a cracked pipe, a separated fitting, or a section that has sunk. They can look alike from indoors, but the labor, materials, and repair scope change once we inspect the line.

Camera inspection and locating

We start at the cleanout when one is available and run a sewer camera through the pipe. The camera shows root masses, cracks, standing water, offset joints, and collapsed sections that are not visible from the bathroom or yard.

An initial camera inspection and surface locating commonly takes 30 to 60 minutes. We check the pipe diameter, mark the approximate route, and identify the distance to the damaged area using the camera counter and locator.

A loose cleanout cap or accessible fitting may be replaced during the same visit. Excavation, under-slab pipe work, or repairs requiring ordered fittings are planned as a separate job after the damaged section is located.

Pipe depth and cleanout access

Pipe material changes the repair plan. PVC can often be cut and coupled at the damaged section, while aging cast iron, clay, and Orangeburg pipe may have failures beyond the first visible break.

Access also affects the work. A cleanout beside an open yard gives us a different route than a line below concrete, landscaping, a fence, or a driveway.

A deep pipe run, roots around the line, and a blocked or damaged cleanout can add work before the pipe is reached. Permit requirements, inspection sign-off, and special-order fittings can also extend the scope of a sewer repair.

Cleanout location and pipe material

Before calling, check whether you can find the sewer cleanout in the yard, garage, utility room, or near the exterior wall. Let us know which fixtures back up, including toilets, tubs, showers, or floor drains.

If sewage is still moving through a drain, tell us where it appears and whether the problem follows laundry, showers, or flushing. A prior camera report, repair invoice, or note about PVC, cast iron, or clay pipe can help us bring suitable fittings.

Tell us about gates, locked side yards, concrete, or landscaping above the suspected pipe route. Call Round Rock Plumbing Company at (512) 882-1291 before a sewer backup spreads through another drain or floor fixture.
